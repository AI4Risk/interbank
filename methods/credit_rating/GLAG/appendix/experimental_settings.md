# Detailed experimental settings

**Representative entities and node sets.** The 105 SEC--EDGAR-accessible banks constitute the supplementary set $\mathcal{V}_{s}$ and provide observed supplementary information. These institutions include banks with investment-banking activities and banks occupying hub roles in the interbank network, making their disclosures informative anchor signals for latent relationship discovery and downstream credit assessment. The three institutions illustrated in [Supplementary information examples](supplementary_information.md) are selected from this set to represent these roles. At each rolling step, $\mathcal{V}_{u}$ denotes the early-disclosure nodes used as few-shot samples for model updating, while $\mathcal{V}_{r}=\mathcal{V}\setminus\mathcal{V}_{u}$ contains the nodes for rating prediction. The memberships of $\mathcal{V}_{u}$ and $\mathcal{V}_{r}$ remain fixed across the 32 quarters.

**Chronological evaluation protocol.** At each rolling step, the complete snapshot from the preceding quarter is used for pre-training. In the current quarter, a class-balanced few-shot strategy uses the early-disclosure nodes $\mathcal{V}_{u}$ for adaptation, and the remaining nodes $\mathcal{V}_{r}$ are held out for prediction. The current-quarter labels of $\mathcal{V}_{u}$ provide the fine-tuning targets, whereas the target-quarter labels of $\mathcal{V}_{r}$ are reserved for evaluation.

**Information-access control.** Supplementary information is aligned with its reporting period: each rolling step uses the corresponding quarterly report or, when applicable, the corresponding semiannual or annual report. The target-quarter labels of $\mathcal{V}_{r}$ are reserved for evaluation and do not participate in node-feature construction, supplementary-input construction, graph updating, or fine-tuning. Each supplementary record describes only its reporting bank. Later-quarter reports are excluded from earlier rolling steps, preserving the chronological information boundary throughout training and evaluation.

**Supplementary coverage and traceability.** The additional-information ratio in the additional-information coverage figure in the manuscript is computed for each bank as the proportion of supplementary financial concepts absent from its fixed-schema original features. For summary generation, the LLM receives only bank-specific facts from the corresponding reporting-period file, and the prompt requires the financial summary and interbank lending outlook to be grounded in these facts. Each output concludes with the reference facts used for both sections. In the anonymized Bank B example in [Supplementary information examples](supplementary_information.md), for instance, `f63` identifies an `InvestmentBankingRevenue` fact retained in the output's reference-fact list.
